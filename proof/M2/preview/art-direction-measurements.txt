WHAT CHANGED IN blender/bonsai_growth.py
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) FRAMING - composed for the duet slot, not as a standalone portrait.
    The camera is now SOLVED rather than padded.  Two targets, TARGET_POT_RIM_V
    = 0.90 and TARGET_CANOPY_TOP_V = 0.45, determine frame_h outright:
        frame_h = (canopy_top_z - pot_rim_z) / (0.90 - 0.45)
    so the tree occupies exactly 45% of the frame height and sits at the same
    height in it as the ASCII tree does in its 40-row panel (v 0.455..0.905).
    FRAME_MARGIN (1.05 -> 1.18) and the new SIDE_MARGIN_MIN are now only guards
    on the horizontal, which the vertical solve does not constrain; CONTENT_BIAS
    is gone, because frame_bottom is derived, not chosen.
    LENS_MM 85, CAM_YAW_DEG 4, CAM_ELEV_DEG 4 are unchanged, as required.

    The floor is now SIZED FROM THE CAMERA.  build_camera reports where the
    bottom edge of the frame lands on the floor plane (y = -0.1441 m) and
    build_floor makes the pool's semi-axis toward the lens 80% of that, so there
    is always a genuinely black band of floor before the last row of pixels.
    Two further fixes there: the floor's outer specular stop went 0.015 -> 0.000
    with its knee moved out to 0.55 (F0 = 0.015 under 100 W of rim is what AgX
    was lifting into the grey band), and a latent bug in the Mapping node - it
    scales BEFORE it translates, so scaling Z by 0 and then adding -floor_z put
    a constant 0.09 m offset into a "flat" radial gradient - was removed.

(2) AMBIENT HUE - blue -> desaturated green-cyan, echoing the phosphor.
    World ramp, backdrop-card ramp and floor ramp all moved to G >= B with B - R
    only just positive.  RimCool and RimCoolLow are now (0.55, 0.95, 0.85) and
    Fill is (0.60, 0.80, 0.75).  Because that gel is 1.33x brighter per watt
    than the blue it replaces, the wattages came DOWN to keep the same amount of
    light: RimCool 108 -> 80 W (80 x 0.857 = 68.6 vs 108 x 0.646 = 69.8),
    RimCoolLow 48 -> 40 W.  The backdrop's bright stop keeps its old LUMINANCE
    (~0.035 linear); only its hue moved, because the glow's job - giving the
    canopy something to be a silhouette against - is a brightness job.
    KeyWarm (22 W, size 0.25, 1.000/0.760/0.520) and PotKick are UNTOUCHED.

(3) SECONDARY MATURATION - the hour is alive now (BRIEF N7).
    Skeleton reach, chain birth steps and blossom timing are untouched; they are
    the sync contract.  What was added rides on top of them, per leaf SITE, on
    that site's own clock from its own birth to MATURE_END = 0.85:
      - size:   cards unfurl to LEAF_UNFURL_FLOOR = 0.55 over LEAF_STEPS as
                before, then ease to 1.0 by MATURE_END.
      - count:  LEAF_CARDS_AT_BIRTH = 4 of the 8 cards open with the pad; the
                other 4 are gated on a per-card hash spread to LEAF_GATE_MAX,
                so a pad thickens ~4 -> 8 cards over its own maturation.
      - colour: a per-vertex "leafage" float drives a mix between a spring
                yellow-green flush and the existing deep-green noise ramp, so
                new growth arrives pale and hardens off by t~0.8.
      - girth:  THICKEN_GAMMA 0.60 -> 1.50.  The old value was a DECELERATING
                curve that spent 66% of the girth range before half time.
    MATURE_LINEAR = 0.30 blends a straight line into the smootherstep, because
    smootherstep has zero slope at BOTH ends and a maturation curve that goes
    flat at t=0.85 is just a later still image.  Every factor is non-negative
    and non-decreasing in t, so the product is: geometry still cannot shrink.

(4) LEAF SPECULAR - Roughness 0.50 -> 0.68, Specular 0.28 -> 0.14.
    The 0.34 translucent mix is untouched and is what carries the rim; the
    specular lobe was only ever competing with the blossoms.
