                  that trace identify the branch type, the life band, the spawn
                  site and the iteration boundary. Segment positions are read from
                  the frozen walk's own write log (growth.steps[i][0]), never
                  recomputed. No growth logic is reimplemented here.

Coordinates     : cbonsai treeWin space. x in [0,80), y in [0,36), y=0 TOP,
                  y=35 the ground row. treeWin origin is stdscr (0,0), so the map
                  into the 80x40 terminal is the identity; terminal rows 36-39 are
                  the base art. The trunk is seeded at (row 35, col 40).
