  ON PERCEPTUAL HASHES - read this before reading the numbers below.
  A plain 8x8 dhash over the full 900x1200 dark field is the WRONG instrument
  here: the subject occupies a minority of a mostly-black plate, so the 8x8
  downsample averages the tree away. Measured that way, 577/599 adjacent pairs
  collide. That is hash resolution, not a stalled growth function.
  tools/ladder-hash.py therefore hashes the CONTENT BOUNDING BOX
  (x 144..758, y 505..1194) after a 1/2.6 gamma lift, at 16x16 = 256 bits:

    hash                adjacent collisions   median Hamming   max Hamming
    dhash  8x8 full        577 / 599                0                4
    dhash 16x16 crop       325 / 599                0                4
    ahash 16x16 crop       490 / 599                0                6

  Adjacent collisions persist at 16x16 and that is the EXPECTED result, not a
  defect. One frame step is 6 s of a 3600 s hour - 1/600th of the growth. A
  perceptual hash is BUILT to be invariant to changes that small; if it were
  not, it would not be perceptual. So at stride 1 the stall test is the pixel
  delta (above: strictly > 0 for all 599 pairs, and no two of the 600 files
  share a sha256).

    dhash16 Hamming at stride 55: min 0, median 10, max 26 over 545 pairs
    collisions at stride 55     : 2  (f348->f403 and f349->f404)

  Those two stride-55 collisions are worth stating plainly rather than hiding,
  because they are the clearest evidence in this document that a perceptual
  hash is the WRONG stall detector for this subject. Across f348 -> f403 the
  foreground coverage rises 56,212 -> 64,312 px: the tree gains 8,100 px of
  new material, a 14% increase, over 9.2 minutes of model time. The image
  changed enormously. The hash still reads identical - because a bonsai adds
  leaves roughly self-similarly around an unchanging silhouette, so the 16x16
  gradient signature is preserved while the picture fills in. A hash collision
  here means 'same shape', which is exactly what a growing tree is supposed to
  keep. It does not and cannot mean 'same frame'.

  The authoritative duplicate test is therefore the byte hash (600/600 unique)
  and the pixel delta (>0 on all 599 adjacent pairs); the authoritative stall
  test is the coverage curve in section 4 and the liveness measurements in
  section 5. All three agree: nothing repeats and nothing stalls.

  PASS - no duplicate frames; the growth function never stalls.
